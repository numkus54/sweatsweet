SweatSweet
pH test kit

Reading the pH of sweat by color, with a pigment taken from purple sweet potato peel.

Abstract

Anthocyanin, the pigment of purple sweet potato, changes color with pH. SweatSweet extracts it from peel that would otherwise be discarded and uses it as a natural indicator for sweat pH, read against a printed color scale. The reference scale on this page is illustrative; final thresholds will be set from experimental data.

    Principle

    2Why the color changes

    Anthocyanin is the pigment that makes purple sweet potato purple. Its color depends on the acidity around it, so the same extract can look pink, purple, blue or green. Matching the color to a printed scale gives a reading without electronics.
